GST Return Filing

Monthly and quarterly GST returns prepared, reconciled and filed on time, with input credit matched against GSTR-2B before submission.

Overview

What GST Return Filing involves

GST is unforgiving about dates: late fees accrue per day, and a mismatch between what your suppliers filed and what you claimed becomes a notice months later. We prepare returns from your sales and purchase data, reconcile input credit against GSTR-2B before filing rather than after, and file ahead of the due date so interest never starts running.

In Detail

Exactly what is covered

Every part of the work, spelled out — so you know what you are paying for before you commit.

01

GSTR-1 outward supplies

Invoice-level reporting of sales, exports, credit notes and amendments, filed monthly or quarterly under QRMP as suits your turnover.

02

GSTR-3B summary return

Tax liability computed, input credit claimed and payment challan prepared, with the return filed on time.

03

GSTR-2B reconciliation

Your purchase register matched against what suppliers actually filed, so you claim only credit that will survive scrutiny — and chase suppliers who have not filed.

04

QRMP and PMT-06

Quarterly filers handled properly, including the monthly tax payments that still fall due in between.

05

Annual return GSTR-9 and 9C

Annual reconciliation prepared where turnover requires it, tying returns back to your books.

06

Notice and mismatch support

If a discrepancy notice arrives, we reconstruct the position and draft the reply.

Avoid These

Common mistakes we prevent

The errors that quietly cost the most — and what we do differently.

01

Claiming credit without matching 2B

Credit not reflected in GSTR-2B is routinely disallowed, with interest. Matching before filing avoids reversing it later.

We check for this
02

Treating nil periods as nothing to do

A nil return is still a return. Late fees accrue on nil filings exactly the same way.

We check for this
03

Ignoring supplier non-compliance

Your credit depends on your suppliers filing. Nobody tells you when they have not, so it has to be checked.

We check for this
04

Missing QRMP monthly payments

Quarterly filing does not mean quarterly payment. PMT-06 still falls due monthly and attracts interest if missed.

We check for this
