Who we are?

A software studio that turns ideas into impactful digital products

Founded in 2024 by Deepak Kumar, Vegavat is a software development company that empowers startups and businesses to create impactful digital products. Our core services include mobile app development, web development, UI/UX design, graphic design and dedicated developer hiring to support your team's growth at every stage.

We bring hands-on experience from diverse industries, prioritising a deep understanding of your business needs to deliver practical, structured solutions designed for lasting performance. As your true technology partner, our team aligns strategy, design and development to help you advance with confidence.

How We Work

Proven processes tailored to every project

By selecting the optimal methodology for every project, we facilitate precise planning, steady progress and reliable delivery from inception to completion.

01
Week 1–2

Discovery & Planning

We understand your business inside-out. Detailed workshops with stakeholders to map current processes, define goals and lock the scope.

02
Week 2–3

Product Roadmap

We translate requirements into a clear roadmap with milestones, features and a sharp MVP definition that guides the build from day one.

03
Week 3–6

UI/UX Design

Designers craft wireframes, user flows and interactive prototypes, you see exactly how the product looks and feels before a line of code.

04
Week 6–10

Development

Engineering runs in Agile sprints with regular demo builds. Features are tested and refined incrementally, never all at the end.

05
Week 10–12
