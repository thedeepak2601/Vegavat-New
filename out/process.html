Our Process

A structured methodology that delivers

Our proven 6-step process ensures successful delivery with clear milestones, timelines and deliverables, no guesswork, no surprises.

Step by Step

From idea to launch in six clear steps

Every phase has defined activities and tangible deliverables, so you always know what's happening and what you'll receive.

01Step 1 · Week 1–2

Discovery & Planning

We understand your business inside-out. Detailed workshops with stakeholders to map current processes, define goals and lock the scope.

Key Activities

  • Stakeholder interviews & workshops
  • Competitor and market research
  • Business requirement gathering
  • Project scope definition
  • Timeline and milestone planning

Deliverables

  • Requirements Document (BRD)
  • Project Plan
02Step 2 · Week 2–3

Product Roadmap

We translate requirements into a clear roadmap with milestones, features and a sharp MVP definition that guides the build from day one.

Key Activities

  • Feature prioritization (MoSCoW)
  • MVP scope definition
  • Tech stack selection
  • System architecture blueprint
  • Sprint planning

Deliverables

  • Product Roadmap
  • Technical Architecture Doc
03Step 3 · Week 3–6

UI/UX Design

Designers craft wireframes, user flows and interactive prototypes, you see exactly how the product looks and feels before a line of code.

Key Activities

  • User research & personas
  • Wireframing & user flows
  • Interactive Figma prototypes
  • Design system & style guide
  • Usability testing

Deliverables

  • Clickable Prototype
  • Design System
04Step 4 · Week 6–10

Development

Engineering runs in Agile sprints with regular demo builds. Features are tested and refined incrementally, never all at the end.

Key Activities

  • Frontend & backend development
  • API & third-party integrations
  • Sprint demos & reviews
  • Code reviews & CI pipelines
  • Incremental QA

Deliverables

  • Working Builds each sprint
  • Integration Documentation
05Step 5 · Week 10–12

Testing & QA

Functional, performance, UI and security testing across devices and OS versions. Your team validates the system during UAT.

Key Activities

  • Test plan & scenarios
  • Automated & manual testing
  • Performance & security audits
  • Bug fixing & refinement
  • User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

Deliverables

  • QA & Test Report
  • UAT Sign-off
06Step 6 · Week 12+

Launch & Support

Go-live to the App Store, Play Store or cloud with data cutover, parallel run and intensive post-launch hypercare for smooth operations.

Key Activities

  • Production deployment
  • Store submission / data cutover
  • Performance monitoring
  • Hypercare support
  • Knowledge transfer

Deliverables

  • Live Product
  • Support Handover
How We Work

Our methodologies, step by step

By selecting the optimal methodology for every project, we facilitate precise planning, steady progress and reliable delivery from inception to completion.

Methodology 1

Agile Development

We organize work into short, focused phases for structured yet adaptable progress. Each phase undergoes thorough review to enable early improvements and maintain precise alignment with your goals.

1

Sprint Planning

We define clear goals and scope for each short, focused sprint, so every cycle moves the product forward with purpose.

→
2

Build & Iterate

Features are developed incrementally with working builds each cycle, never saved up for one risky push at the end.

→
3

Review & Demo

Every sprint ends with a demo so you give feedback early and we validate progress against your goals.

→
4

Retrospective & Refine

We capture learnings after each sprint and feed them straight into the next, improving with every cycle.

Methodology 2

Kanban Workflow

Kanban visualizes tasks moving through a clear, transparent board from start to finish. Teams gain full visibility, cut delays, spot bottlenecks instantly and prioritize dynamically.

1

Visualize the Board

Every task is mapped across clear stages, from backlog to done, giving the whole team full visibility at a glance.

→
2

Limit Work in Progress

We cap how many tasks are active at once, keeping flow steady and the team focused on finishing, not just starting.

→
3

Manage the Flow

We track how work moves through the board, spotting bottlenecks instantly and unblocking them before they cause delays.

→
4

Continuously Improve

Board metrics guide dynamic prioritization, so the process keeps getting faster and smoother over time.
